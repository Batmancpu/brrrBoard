brrBoard — Your keyboard, thoughtfully made.

Created by MangoLoads, brrBoard brings fast, familiar Android typing together with a refined frosted-glass visual style, thoughtful motion and local-first tools. It is built on the open-source work of FrostKeys, HeliBoard, OpenBoard and AOSP LatinIME, with upstream credits and licenses preserved.

Designed for everyday typing
• Frosted keyboard, Toolbar, Emoji and Clipboard surfaces with independently controlled materials.
• English and Hinglish layouts, suggestions and custom personal words.
• Searchable Emoji, local Clipboard history, editing shortcuts and keyboard settings.
• Optional compatible offline gesture-typing library import.
• Local backup and restore for your settings and supported data.

Privacy, without confusing promises
brrBoard itself does not request Android internet permission. Settings, personal dictionaries, learned words and Clipboard history stay on your device. Its microphone uses a private English recognition model when that model is provisioned. Audio recording starts only when you ask for it. The ordinary signed update reuses the model stored privately by a previous model-inclusive bootstrap/recovery build; model weights are not automatically downloaded to your phone.

Your manually exported backup ZIP may contain personal words and Clipboard content. It is not encrypted; store it privately.

Stable updates
The current Android package is com.mangoloads.board. Original-signed releases are available through GitHub and can be followed in Obtainium. A former package, com.orion.frostkeys.brrrboard, is a separate app and needs an explicit export/restore migration rather than an in-place update.

brrBoard is free, open-source and does not include advertising or in-app donations.
