English-only output is unresolved until Dictate itself uses English and the owner verifies it on Reno10. Keep brrBoard free of INTERNET permission; Dictate may use its own configured provider/network.
- **Themes shipped in v1.0.23:** System-following OG dark/light, a single opt-in Apple-palette action-key tint, neutral glass and ordinary keycaps, legacy tint migration. Reno10 tint contrast/appearance is not yet accepted.

They show a three-slot suggestions row, center confidence fill, a separate compact right-side accessory, QWERTY rows, an outlined idle Shift glyph, and Emoji/mic at opposite lower corners. Do not inspect or claim frame-by-frame review of the YouTube video. Never commit raw images or typed/clipboard content.
- **Other fixed source behavior, device acceptance pending:** v1.0.19 reports the measured IME host-content boundary, calibrates portrait height, keeps the footer in toolbar mode, and uses 300ms mic hold switching. Alphabetic 123 tap switches to Symbols; hold opens numpad. Emoji hold-and-release menu remains three choices; native Android/ColorOS owns hide animation. Treat these as code facts, not Reno10 acceptance.
- **Geometry constraint:** The owner uses no visible navigation bar and wants the useless bottom gap removed without crowding the Emoji/mic footer. Current code reserves a system bottom inset after the 38dp footer and reports it in the host boundary. Measure actual Reno10 insets/content boundary and panel heights before changing it; do not simply zero system insets.
- **Suggestions and proofreading:** The strip already has three slots and uses decoder-owned `mWillAutoCorrect` for center emphasis. Do not invent ranking weights. Android SpellCheckerService provides word-level spelling suggestions, not Apple's Writing Tools grammar Proofread. Keep the compact accessory distinct; no AI/cloud proofreader.
- **Still open:** Stable layout across letters/symbols/numpad/Emoji/Clipboard/toolbar, stale tint transition test, icon audit beyond Shift, candidate quality, finite `brrPersonal` Romanized Hindi/Hinglish vocabulary with profanity/slang valid-only, bounded local learning and emoji mappings, Smart Emoji up to three rows, QuickPath rights/native compatibility, English-only Dictate setup and device acceptance. Do not add the 116MB Apple Emoji font or the unlicensed/unverified GlideLib binary.
- **Research and source baseline:** OPPO publishes Reno10 5G at 2412×1080 and 394ppi, but runtime dp density, ColorOS build, display scaling, and nav insets are device-only. Existing dictionary/Ngram/proximity decoding is the baseline; the earlier arbitrary weighted score was not validated. The exact draft 252-word list from the unavailable prior working tree cannot be recovered.
