brrBoard production offline context data, version brr-context-1

English n-gram observations derive from the Tatoeba CC0 sentence export.
https://tatoeba.org/en/downloads
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Roman Hindi and mixed n-gram observations derive from the following datasets
curated by the Lingo Research Group at IIT Gandhinagar. CC BY 4.0 applies.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
PHINC: https://huggingface.co/datasets/LingoIITGN/PHINC/tree/44b5471df75e508b9d6fcdbcc93df04bb1e84056
COMI-LINGUA: https://huggingface.co/datasets/LingoIITGN/COMI-LINGUA/tree/0214cb358e59e36da60e01d3d59ddd24df897749
HinGE: https://huggingface.co/datasets/LingoIITGN/HinGE/tree/e25a10382d9adb77f3a4f03b721fbd0a43c0de4c

The distributed model contains filtered, aggregated and quantized short
n-grams and authored usage examples. Public input revisions and digests,
data transformations and replay are available in tools/dictionary/.
