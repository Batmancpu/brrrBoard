## Diagnosed paths and bounded fixes

1. `BoardFooterView.emojiTapUp` deliberately posted each ordinary first tap after `ViewConfiguration.getDoubleTapTimeout()` (normally hundreds of milliseconds) to distinguish privacy double taps. First valid Emoji and ABC taps now call the existing click immediately on `ACTION_UP`. Spatially close complete tap pairs are still consumed disjointly; opening Emoji then deliberately double-tapping toggles the established manual-incognito preference and returns to ABC. A second fast ABC tap is consumed instead of reopening a just-closed panel. Long-press shortcut arc, drag cancellation, touch exploration/accessibility click, and protected-mode checks are unchanged. Follow-up device test must cover an intentional rapid Emoji→ABC sequence near the double-tap threshold, because those gestures share one target.
2. `KeyboardSwitcher.transitionToPanel` used to set the incoming Emoji view's alpha to zero and animate it back to one. Emoji now appears at full opacity as soon as the panel action completes; return from Emoji to ABC already had a flash-free immediate path. Other panel transitions retain their motion preset. No new effect, blur, model or texture is introduced.
3. The fixed category row lives **inside** `emoji_grid_shell`; `strip_container` and any persistent typing Emoji row become `GONE` for Emoji. `KeyboardSwitcher.setEmojiKeyboard` now samples the real visible alphabet body, external strip and optional persistent row **before** hiding them, and gives their total height/width to the compact Emoji view. `EmojiPalettesView.onMeasure` reconciles this same-width hint with the existing saved typing frame and clamps it to available height. This fills the top gap on first open even when an OEM has not populated the wrapper's previous measured-frame memory. Do not stretch a stale portrait height into landscape/one-handed widths or expand Search/Expanded Browse by accident.
4. Removed the redundant early `updatePersistentEmojiRow` while entering Emoji. The final update still hides it after the panel is visible, reducing unnecessary candidate-row work and one transient wrong-state toggle.
