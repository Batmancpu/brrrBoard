## What remains unproved / potential Android-specific failure modes

1. **Cross-app, cross-UID** pipe and binder transfer: real brrBoard `com.mangoloads.board` as mic recorder and brrSpeak `com.mangoloads.speak` as recognition engine. Same-UID separate-process success does not prove Android package/service visibility, authorization, descriptor handoff, receiver lifecycle, or precise callback behavior across UIDs on ColorOS 15.
2. Android 15/ColorOS microphone permission/lifecycle: foreground IME recording must have `RECORD_AUDIO`, user consent and appropriate app/IME lifecycle; app must not start forbidden background recording. brrSpeak receiver must only read the supplied fd, never start a second microphone capture. Ensure exceptions, service disconnect, binder death, rotation/IME dismissal, and app background restrictions close resources safely. Don't promise universal device compatibility before owner real-device test.
3. **Gesture collision is real and must be explicitly resolved:** brrBoard now uses an idle **double-tap mic** to switch keyboards, while proposed voice interaction is tap mic to begin recording, tap again to stop. Two fast taps can represent either request, and Android `GestureDetector` confirmed-single-tap may defer the first action during the double-tap recognition interval. **Do not claim both instant start/stop and unchanged fast double-tap switch are simultaneously disambiguated** without a documented gesture precedence/timing rule and user acceptance. Preserve switch feature unless owner explicitly chooses a replacement; show tradeoffs at GO.
4. Recognition provider selection: verify brrSpeak is discoverable/bound as the **explicit** service, with exact package/component and no accidental fallback to purchased Dictate (original and fork are both installed). Separate installed services do not mean two microphones must capture; only keyboard captures in proposed external-source mode. Test external provider choice and errors.
5. End-of-utterance: precise PCM format, fd lifetime/ownership, EOF when second mic tap stops, no blocking UI thread, bounded buffers, cancellation, backpressure, timeouts, late/stale callbacks and partial/final result handling. Prevent duplicate inserts, accidental text loss, mic indicator stuck ON, or a recorder continuing after IME disappearance. Avoid waking activity/popups, keyboard replacement or unnecessary notification. Error 9 should surface actionable diagnostics, not be swallowed or falsely labeled fixed.
6. APK/signing: preserve original brrBoard pinned signer for Obtainium and monotonic version; don't confuse stable vs ephemeral brrSpeak APK signatures; maintain distinct app IDs; do not silently change default voice service or user's existing provider keys.
7. Provider availability/network/API keys: the experimental brrSpeak backend successfully transcribed because the owner had configured it. Confirm settings restored/available in the target build, no credential migration or logging, appropriate offline/online expectations for selected provider.
