# Suggestion animation presets, playground and capture audio focus — 2026-11-26

Owner requested all three research directions together, selectable in Settings, a test playground, and background audio pause during both normal tap and hold-to-talk mic capture.

## Implementation
- Keep the v1.0.361 clipped QuickType renderer and fixed-width confidence chrome. Add a separate preference `suggestion_motion_style`: Scale (default, strong character growth/contraction and contiguous unmatched-run spacing), Spring (analytical critically damped x/y position and inherited velocity, softer scale), Ripple (staggered glyph growth/contraction, bounded to 30 ms), Classic (previous dissolve). These are original adaptations of the techniques researched in LTMorphingLabel, HTextView and TOMSMorphingLabel plus Apple spring guidance, not direct library ports or exact private Apple algorithms. No new library dependency. Preserve existing global Previous Default, animator-disabled and power-saving behavior.
- Motion Settings now provides a persistent selector and a fixed sample replay drawn by the real QuickTypeMotion renderer. The live IME tryout directly below remains private, no personalized learning or microphone. No user content is used by the deterministic sample.
- Actual PCM worker paths KeyboardPcmAudioSource and KeyboardRecordedAudioSource acquire one best-effort AUDIOFOCUS_GAIN_TRANSIENT_EXCLUSIVE lease before startRecording and abandon in the microphone release finally block, including failures, cancellation, duration caps, and editor loss. No focus held while only transcription runs. No stream-volume mutation, media-key injection, DND, foreground-service or permission changes. Android 15+ targeting API 35+ may deny requests by apps that are not top app or foreground service; other players may ignore focus. Do not claim universal mute/pause. Existing capture remains functional if denied.
- Research: https://github.com/lexrus/LTMorphingLabel ; https://github.com/hanks-zyh/HTextView ; https://github.com/tomknig/TOMSMorphingLabel ; https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10158/ ; https://developer.android.com/reference/android/media/AudioFocusRequest ; https://developer.android.com/media/optimize/audio-focus .
