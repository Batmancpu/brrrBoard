# brrBoard privacy policy

brrBoard uses local dictionaries and preferences for typing and does not request Android internet access. Contacts, if enabled and authorized, are used for local suggestions. Clipboard history and learned suggestions can be managed in settings.

When you tap the microphone, brrBoard uses Android’s currently selected system speech-recognition service and inserts the returned transcript into the focused field. The selected service controls transcription. In the maintainer’s setup, this is DictateKeyboard, which may send recorded audio to the provider/API configured inside that separate app. Review that provider’s settings and privacy terms. brrBoard does not receive or store its API credentials, and its own APK has no network permission. Mic tap does not switch to a separate voice-input keyboard or popup. External links open in another application.
